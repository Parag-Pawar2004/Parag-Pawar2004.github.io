Quantum Traffic Gridlock: QUBO and depth-4 QAOA for signal control

A scenario-independent QUBO for two linked intersections, built by projecting the exact queue recursion onto degree-2 Walsh functions while keeping the switching cost exact. Eight qubits, depth-4 QAOA, 300 objective evaluations, 4096 shots. On the public scenario the surrogate correlates 0.995 with the official cost and its minimiser is the true optimum. Over 150 random scenarios the mean surrogate regret is 1.2%. The report claims no quantum advantage.
